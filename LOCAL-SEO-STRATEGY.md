## Template Components

Each page will include:

1. **Hero Section**
   - H1: "[Service] in [City], TX | Creative Constructors"
   - Location-specific imagery
   - Quick CTA (phone + book)

2. **Service Details**
   - What we do (service-specific)
   - Why choose us for this service in this location
   - Specific problems we solve

3. **Location Benefits**
   - Response time for this area
   - Local service areas/neighborhoods
   - Location-specific testimonials if available

4. **Trust Signals**
   - 15+ years experience
   - Licensed & insured
   - Same-day service
   - 2-hour response time

5. **Related Services** (internal linking)
   - Other services in this location
   - This service in nearby locations

6. **Structured Data**
   - Service schema
   - LocalBusiness schema with service-specific focus
   - Breadcrumbs

7. **Strong CTAs**
   - Multiple contact points
   - Emergency service callout
   - Free quote emphasis

## SEO Benefits

- **Long-tail keywords**: "electrician university park tx", "plumber highland park"
- **Local intent capture**: People searching "[service] near me" in those areas
- **Internal linking**: Cross-link services and locations
- **Reduced bounce rate**: Exact match content for search intent
- **Local pack rankings**: Google prioritizes service-specific pages
- **Voice search**: "Find an electrician in University Park"
